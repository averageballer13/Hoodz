How Hoodz Loans works

  1. Deposit gHOODZ into a Cooler you own. Nobody else can move it.
  2. The Clearinghouse lends 2,894.12 reserve per gHOODZ — deliberately below liquid backing, so the Treasury is never underwater.
  3. Repay any amount at any time to release the matching slice of collateral.
  4. Miss the expiry and the Treasury claims the collateral. That is the whole downside — no oracle, no cascade, no penalty.
